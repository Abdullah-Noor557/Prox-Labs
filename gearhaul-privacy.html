Gearhaul Privacy Policy

Gearhaul: Driving & Truck Sim (Android package com.gearhaul.game)
Effective 14 October 2026

Gearhaul is a mobile game published by Prox Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the game.

The short version

  • You do not need an account to play, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device only.
  • The game shows adverts through Google AdMob and sells optional items through Google Play. Those Google services process some data to do their job, as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

  • Game progress and settings: your vehicles, money, jobs, purchases and options are saved in the game's own storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it.
  • Pictures you choose for decals: if you add your own picture to a vehicle, you pick it with your device's system picker. The game copies only that picture into its own storage on your device. It is not uploaded, and the game does not ask for access to your photo library.

Adverts and your choices

  • Rewarded adverts are always optional: you choose to watch one in return for something in the game.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised adverts are shown, and you can change that choice later from the game's settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised adverts, in your device settings under Google > Ads.
  • Buying "No Adverts" or the Driver Club in the game removes the adverts shown between sessions.

Permissions

The game asks only for internet access, which is needed for adverts and purchases. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Gearhaul is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

Keeping and deleting data

We do not hold your game data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to keep. To delete your game data, clear the game's storage in your device settings or uninstall the game. For data held by Google, use the controls in your Google account or contact Google.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Because the data described above is processed by Google rather than held by us, many requests are best made through your Google account, but you can always contact us and we will help where we can.

Security

Game data stays in the game's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read on a standard device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.
